# Northstar Dynamics Remote Work Policy

Policy ID: NS-HR-REMOTE-001

Effective date: 2026-01-01

## Eligibility

Full-time employees who have completed 90 calendar days of employment may request hybrid work. Contractors, interns, and employees in roles requiring continuous on-site access need written approval from their department vice president.

## Standard schedule

Eligible employees may work remotely up to two days per work week. Teams designate at least two common in-office collaboration days. A manager may temporarily require on-site attendance for customer meetings, incident response, regulated work, or essential training with reasonable notice when circumstances permit.

## Location and security

Remote work is permitted only from a location within the employee's approved country of employment unless Global Mobility and Information Security approve an exception in writing. Employees must use a company-managed device, the corporate identity gateway, multi-factor authentication, and the approved secure network connection. Company confidential data may not be printed or stored on personal devices.

## Review

Managers review hybrid arrangements every six months. Remote eligibility may be suspended for documented security violations or when role responsibilities materially change. Suspension is not automatic discipline and must follow the employee review process.
