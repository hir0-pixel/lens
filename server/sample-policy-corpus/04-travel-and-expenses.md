# Northstar Dynamics Travel and Expense Policy

Policy ID: NS-FIN-TRAVEL-004

Effective date: 2026-01-01

## Approval

Business travel requires manager approval before booking. International travel also requires the cost-center owner and Corporate Security risk review. Travelers must book through the approved travel platform unless an emergency makes it unavailable.

## Air and rail

Economy class is required for flights under six hours. Premium economy is permitted for flights of six hours or more with manager approval. Business class requires a documented medical accommodation or executive vice-president approval. Reasonable rail travel is reimbursable when it is cost-effective.

## Lodging and meals

Travelers should select a safe hotel within the published city rate cap. Itemized receipts are required for lodging and for any individual expense of 25 US dollars or more, subject to stricter local requirements. Alcohol is reimbursable only with a customer meal, within the meal cap, and where lawful.

## Submission

Expense reports must be submitted within 15 calendar days after travel ends. The employee certifies that expenses are accurate, business-related, and not reimbursed elsewhere. Managers and Finance may request supporting documentation but must not approve their own expenses.
