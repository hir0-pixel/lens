# Northstar Dynamics Information Security and Data Handling Policy

Policy ID: NS-SEC-DATA-003

Effective date: 2026-01-01

## Classification

Information is classified as Public, Internal, Confidential, or Restricted. Restricted information includes credentials, private cryptographic keys, regulated personal data, unreleased financial results, and customer secrets designated Restricted by contract.

## Approved systems

Internal, Confidential, and Restricted information may be processed only in approved company systems. Employees must not paste Confidential or Restricted information into consumer AI tools, personal email, public paste sites, or unapproved cloud storage. Approved enterprise AI services enforce identity, access, audit, retention, and data-processing controls.

## Credentials

Passwords, access tokens, API keys, and private keys must be stored in the approved secrets manager. They must not appear in source code, chat messages, tickets, documents, screenshots, or logs. A suspected disclosure must be reported to Security Operations immediately and the affected credential must be rotated.

## Access and retention

Access follows least privilege and is reviewed quarterly for privileged roles. Authorization is checked using current identity, device, resource, and policy state. Retention does not grant access: preserved records remain subject to classification and authorization.

## Incidents

Employees report suspected phishing, malware, unauthorized disclosure, lost managed devices, or unusual account activity through the security incident channel immediately. Employees must not independently investigate another person's account or disable security controls.
