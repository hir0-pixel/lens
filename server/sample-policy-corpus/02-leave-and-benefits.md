# Northstar Dynamics Leave and Benefits Policy

Policy ID: NS-HR-LEAVE-002

Effective date: 2026-01-01

## Annual leave

Full-time employees receive 20 paid annual-leave days per calendar year. Leave accrues monthly. Up to five unused days carry into the next calendar year and expire on March 31 unless local law requires a longer period.

## Requests

Employees should submit planned leave at least ten business days in advance. Managers respond within five business days and may decline particular dates for documented operational coverage, but they should offer a reasonable alternative. Emergencies and illness do not require advance notice; employees notify their manager as soon as reasonably possible.

## Sick leave

Employees receive ten paid sick-leave days per calendar year. Medical documentation may be requested after three consecutive work days of absence where permitted by local law. Managers must not request a diagnosis and must send any medical document directly to People Operations.

## Parental and statutory leave

Northstar provides 16 weeks of paid parental leave to eligible employees after six months of service. Statutory entitlements that are more favorable supersede this policy. People Operations confirms eligibility and coordinates leave privately.
